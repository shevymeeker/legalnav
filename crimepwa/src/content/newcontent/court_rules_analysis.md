# Daviess Circuit Court Rules - Document Relationship Analysis

## Overview

The C6 Local Rules document serves as the procedural framework for the 6th Judicial Circuit, Daviess Circuit Court. It integrates multiple layers of Kentucky legal authority while establishing specific local procedures.

## Document Relationships

### Available Project Files (Direct References)

**1. gov_ky_krs_title_50.odt (KRS Title 50: Domestic Relations)**
   - Rule 5 explicitly references FCRPP 2-9, which in turn relies on KRS Title 50
   - Specific statutory references in local rules:
     * KRS 403.735 (dissolution procedures)
     * KRS 403.270(4) (custody transfers)
     * KRS 403.740(4) (summons reissuance)
     * KRS 405 (child support)
     * KRS 620.110 (immediate entitlement to custody)
     * KRS 620-625 (matters originating from these chapters)

**2. Evidence-Manual-8th-Edition-FINAL.pdf**
   - Rule 6.03 addresses courtroom resources and evidence presentation
   - Governs admission of exhibits, testimonial evidence, documentary evidence
   - Referenced implicitly in Rules 2.01-2.05 regarding pre-trial procedures and motion hearings

### External Referenced Sources (Not in Project)

**1. Kentucky Revised Statutes (KRS) - Beyond Title 50**
   - KRS 199.570, 610.340, 625.045, 625.108 (confidentiality and sealed records)
   - KRS 26A.015 (recusal procedures)
   - Comprehensive statutory foundation for all local rules

**2. Kentucky Rules of Civil Procedure (CR)**
   - CR 7.03(1)(b): Personal identifiers and sealed documents
   - CR 16: Pre-trial conferences (Rule 2.03)
   - CR 23, 50, 56, 59-71: Motion requirements and briefing (Rule 2.02)
   - CR 33, 34, 36: Written discovery procedures (Rule 6.02)

**3. Family Court Rules of Procedure and Practice (FCRPP)**
   - FCRPP 2-9: Core domestic relations procedures (Rule 5)
   - FCRPP 4: Preliminary matters referenced in Rule 5.01
   - FCRPP 4(4): Domestic relations commissioner recommendations
   - FCRPP 5 and 9: Motions and documentation requirements
   - FCRPP 11(2): Contempt proceedings
   - FCRPP 12: Case transfers
   - FCRPP 21: Hearing on motions (Rule 5.02)
   - FCRPP 32-36: Adoptions procedures (Rule 3)
   - FCRPP 7(2): Residency and moving location (Appendix B)

**4. Supreme Court Rules (SCR) and Criminal Rules (RCr)**
   - SCR 1.040(3)(a): Rules adoption authority
   - RCr 13.02: Applicable criminal procedures
   - Cited in Rule 1.02 as foundation for local rules' effectiveness

**5. Rules of Domestic Violence Proceedings (RDDC)**
   - RDDC 5: Additional domestic violence case procedures
   - RDDC 104D(3): Case assignment procedures
   - RDDC 1D(iv)(c): Transfer circumstances
   - Entire Appendix A dedicated to 24-hour DV Protocol

## Structural Organization

### Rule 1: Introduction/Administrative Procedure
- Establishes authority framework
- References SCR and RCr for procedural foundation
- Governs court organization (two divisions with Master and Deputy Master Commissioners)

### Rule 2: Court Scheduling / Motion Hour / Procedures for Filing
- Implements CR 16 pre-trial conference requirements
- Establishes motion docket procedures per CR 23, 50, 56, 59-71
- Sets specific hearing schedules for civil and criminal matters

### Rule 3: Adoptions / Termination of Parental Rights
- Directly implements FCRPP 32-36
- No specific local variations noted

### Rule 4: Domestic Violence Protocol and 24 Hour Accessibility Policy
- Establishes 24-hour access procedures
- References RDDC 5 and joint jurisdiction protocol
- Detailed in Appendix A

### Rule 5: Domestic Relations Practice (FCRPP 2-9)
- Comprehensive adoption of FCRPP 2-9
- Adds local procedures for:
  * Preliminary matters (5.01)
  * Matters currently/previously in district court (5.02)
  * K.R.S. 620.110 petitions (5.03)
  * Motions (5.04)
  * Hearings before Domestic Relations Commissioner (5.05)
  * Decree of dissolution procedures (5.06)
  * Custody/timesharing matters (5.07)

### Rule 6: Miscellaneous Rules Relating to Family Law Practice
- Personal identifiers (6.01): Implements CR 7.03(1)(b)
- Written discovery (6.02): References CR 33, 34, 36
- Courtroom resources (6.03): Evidence presentation standards

### Appendix A: Domestic Violence Protocol
- Comprehensive 24-hour accessibility procedures
- Joint jurisdiction between circuit/family and district courts
- Processing procedures during and after regular business hours
- Contempt proceedings per KRS 403.760 and FCRPP 11(2)

### Appendix B: Timesharing/Visitation Guidelines
- Detailed parenting time schedules
- Holiday allocation formulas
- School break procedures
- Residency and relocation procedures per FCRPP 7(2)

## Critical Interdependencies

**1. Domestic Relations Triangle**
   - Local Rules (procedural framework)
   - FCRPP (statewide family court procedures)
   - KRS Title 50 (statutory authority)

**2. Domestic Violence Chain**
   - RDDC (statewide DV rules)
   - Local Rule 4 (local DV protocol)
   - Appendix A (implementation procedures)
   - KRS 403.760 (contempt authority)

**3. General Civil Procedures**
   - CR (statewide civil procedures)
   - Local Rules 2 and 6 (local modifications)
   - SCR (supreme court oversight)

## Gaps Requiring External Resources

The following sources are referenced extensively but not present in project files:

1. **Family Court Rules of Procedure and Practice (FCRPP)** - Critical for understanding Rules 3, 5, and portions of Rules 2 and 6
2. **Kentucky Rules of Civil Procedure (CR)** - Essential for motions, discovery, and general civil procedures
3. **Rules of Domestic Violence Proceedings (RDDC)** - Necessary to fully implement Rule 4 and Appendix A
4. **Additional KRS Chapters** - Particularly KRS 199 (adoptions), 403 (dissolution), 405 (child support), 610 (records), 620-625 (dependency/neglect)

## Practical Application

For effective use of these local rules:

1. Start with the specific local rule section
2. Identify referenced FCRPP/CR/KRS provisions
3. Cross-reference with available project files (KRS Title 50, Evidence Manual)
4. Consult external sources for complete FCRPP/CR/RDDC procedures
5. Apply local modifications per C6 Local Rules
6. Follow appendix guidelines for DV and timesharing matters
